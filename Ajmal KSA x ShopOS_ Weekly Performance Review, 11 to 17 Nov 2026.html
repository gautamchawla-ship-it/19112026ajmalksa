White Friday lifted revenue 83% and blended ROAS to 3.82x, while spend scaled 76%

  • Shopify revenue reached SAR 87,932 against SAR 47,962, with orders up from 204 to 322. The White Friday Sale (a buy-two-of-the-same-product discount, live on the store from 10 Nov) is the main change.
  • Blended spend rose to SAR 22,998 from SAR 13,087 (Meta SAR 19,693, Google SAR 3,305). Blended ROAS still improved, to 3.82x from 3.67x.
  • Meta returned 2.02x on SAR 19,693, up from 1.60x. LPV-to-ATC almost doubled (10.0% to 18.0%) once the offer creatives went live, and conversion rate rose from 0.31% to 0.42%. CPM rose 59% to SAR 4.48 as auctions heat up into BFCM.
  • Google returned 11.67x, SAR 38,565 of conversion value on SAR 3,305. Brand conversion search carried 60% of that value at 11.8x.
  • Paid Shopify ROAS was 2.05x (Google, Instagram, Facebook, Snapchat and YouTube referrers), against 2.16x. Paid referrers drove 54% of revenue. New customers brought SAR 72,409 (82% of revenue).
  • We expect to land SAR 390,000 to 405,000 of Shopify revenue for November: SAR 173,661 achieved to 17 Nov, SAR 13,500 a day for 18 to 26 Nov and SAR 18,500 a day for the 4 BFCM days. The Targets tab has the build-up.

Meta insights

What the week tells us, and what we are doing next.

Meta: what the week tells us

  • 1. Catalog is the best-returning format; give it more budget. The White Friday catalog campaigns returned 3.34x (TOF) and 2.64x (BOF) on SAR 4,831, and the single catalog overlay ad brought SAR 10,524 at 3.57x. Move the build-phase increase (SAR 700 a day to reach plan) into these two campaigns first.
  • 2. Low-price offer statics convert. Musk Silk 24 ml (Buy 1 Save 50%) returned 3.91x and Al-Arosah powder 3.56x; Kessr Vietnami returned 3.00x in the interest campaign. Musk Silk New is live at 2.18x on SAR 1,290, the biggest static this week.
  • 3. Add a separate discount for gift sets. Gift sets do not sell at full price: the gift set campaign returned 0.46x on SAR 1,049 before it was paused, and the White Friday pair discount rarely fits a gift set. A gift-set discount of its own would show us which gift sets work at a discounted price, and that read gives us the insight we need to plan gift sets for Ramadan.
  • 4. Engaged audiences return most, and the ATC campaign is feeding them. Engaged returned 2.36x against 1.75x for prospecting and 1.71x for existing customers. The ATC-optimised campaign is doing its job here: SAR 614 bought 1,240 link clicks and 276 add to carts at SAR 2.22 each, the cheapest carts in the account, and those shoppers flow into the engaged pool that the BOF catalog and MOF/BOF offer campaigns convert. Judge it on the carts it adds, not its own 0.51x. Existing customers spent SAR 1,134 for SAR 1,939; cap that segment in Advantage+ audience settings.
  • 5. CPM is rising into BFCM. SAR 4.48 this week from 2.81, with 17 Nov at SAR 5.02. Expect it to keep climbing; protect ROAS by spending on the formats that convert (catalog, low-price offer statics) rather than broad reach.

New customers drove the White Friday lift; returning customers bought bigger baskets

  • New customers carried the week. New-customer revenue rose 88% to SAR 72,409 on 284 orders (from 173), and stayed at 82% of Shopify revenue (80% the week before). The buy-two offer and the catalog prospecting campaigns brought in first-time buyers rather than just pulling forward repeat orders.
  • Returning customers spent more per order. Returning revenue rose 65% to SAR 15,523, but on only 7 more orders (31 to 38); their AOV jumped to SAR 408 from SAR 303, against SAR 255 for new customers. The pair offer is lifting basket size for people who already know the brand.
  • What it means for BFCM. With 284 new buyers this week, the post-purchase follow-up matters: a second-order nudge (WhatsApp, email or an existing-customer audience on Meta) before 27 Nov can turn this week's first-timers into BFCM repeat buyers, at the higher returning AOV.

Low-ASP products to put behind the White Friday offer

  • The offer. The store's White Friday Sale has been live since 10 Nov as a same-product pair discount. The ads promote it as “Buy 1 Save 50%”. Buying two of the same product makes the second half price, so each unit costs 75% of list.
  • Why low ASP. Last week's report showed products at SAR 48 to 65 a unit converting at 1.25% of clicks against 0.15% for SAR 190 to 305 gift sets. This week the low-price offer statics returned up to 3.9x while gift sets returned 0.46x.
